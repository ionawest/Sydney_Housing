    📖 Plain-English Guide to Metrics & Terms

    How each official dataset and financial ratio is calculated

    P&I (Principal & Interest)Finance
    A standard home loan repayment where each monthly payment pays down both the interest charged by the bank and a portion of the original loan balance (principal) over 30 years.
    Est. Monthly Ownership Costmortgage
    The true monthly cost of buying a home: your monthly P&I mortgage repayment (after your chosen deposit %) plus an ongoing buffer for council rates, water, and strata levies (~$680/mo for units, ~$420/mo for houses).
    Buy-vs-Rent Cost Ratiobuy_rent_ratio
    Monthly Ownership Cost divided by Monthly Rent. A ratio of 1.0x means buying costs the exact same per month as renting. Below 1.0x means buying is cheaper per month than renting; 2.5x means buying costs 2.5 times as much per month as renting.
    Gross Rental Yield (%)yield
    Annual rent divided by the property's purchase price. Higher yield (e.g., 5%+) means stronger rental cashflow for investors and lower purchase prices relative to rent.
    Price-to-Income AffordabilityAffordability
    The suburb's median property price divided by the median annual household income of locals living there. Lower numbers (e.g., 6x–8x) are more affordable; 15x+ indicates severe price-to-local-income stretch.
    SEIFA (Socio-Economic Index)seifa
    The Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) Index of Relative Socio-economic Advantage and Disadvantage (IRSAD). Scored around a national average of 1,000 and ranked into Deciles 1 to 10 (10 = top 10% most advantaged suburbs in income, education, and housing).
    Strata vs. Non-StrataProperty Type
    NSW Valuer General classification: Strata properties share common walls/land (apartments, units, townhouses), while Non-Strata (Torrens) properties are standalone houses on their own land title.
    Ambient-Adjusted Crime Ratecrime
    BOCSAR residential violent and property incidents per 1,000 people, adjusted for daily commercial/event visitor foot traffic and stripped of train-station Opal fare fines so major hubs (like Sydney CBD or Olympic Park) aren't unfairly skewed.
    Couples Commute Fairnesscouple_diff
    The absolute time gap |W1 - W2| in minutes between Partner A's commute and Partner B's commute. A diff near 0 mins means both partners share an equally fair commute.
    ANEF / Aircraft Flight Noiseflight_noise
    Australian Noise Exposure Forecast corridors measuring low-altitude aircraft approach/departure noise from Sydney Airport (Kingsford Smith) and Western Sydney Airport, scored 0 (quiet) to 100 (directly under low final approach).
    Winsorized Linear Scaling (P2–P98)Formula Math
    Normalizes metrics to a 0 to 1 scale after clipping extreme 2% outliers (like $25M harbour mansions) so a handful of extreme suburbs don't squash the colour scale for the other 98% of Sydney.
    Percentile Rank Scaling (0–1)Formula Math
    Ranks every suburb evenly from 0.00 (lowest in Sydney) to 1.00 (highest in Sydney). A score of 0.85 means the suburb ranks higher than 85% of all Sydney suburbs.
